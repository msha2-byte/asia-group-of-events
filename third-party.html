TRANSPARENCY

Third-Party Services & SDK Audit

Last updated: 8 October 2026. AGE uses third-party services only where they provide a defined website function. Optional services are intended to load only after the visitor chooses optional services in the consent banner.

Google Analytics 4

Used for aggregate website measurement when configured with a valid AGE Measurement ID. The ID is stored in config.js. Replace the placeholder before deployment. AGE should configure its Google Analytics property and privacy settings to match its actual practices.

Instagram embed SDK

Used only to render Instagram post embeds when AGE chooses to add specific post permalinks. The public profile remains a direct link. The Instagram script is loaded only after optional consent.

Google AdSense

The package includes a consent-gated integration point for Google AdSense. It will not display real ads until AGE supplies a valid publisher ID and configures its AdSense account. Personalized advertising may involve additional consent requirements. Google states that publishers serving personalized ads to users in the EEA, UK or Switzerland must use a certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB TCF. citeturn0search4

External images

Optional external imagery is loaded from image hosts such as Unsplash. Images currently used as placeholders are not represented as AGE event photographs. Before launch, AGE should replace them with images it owns or has permission to use, and retain appropriate license/attribution records.

How to update providers

Review this page whenever a new analytics, advertising, social, payment, font, image or embedded-content provider is added. Remove integrations that are no longer needed.
