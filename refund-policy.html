Booking, Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026. Event bookings are customised. The written quotation, booking confirmation and/or signed event contract for the specific event controls the deposit, cancellation, rescheduling and refund terms.

Before payment

AGE should disclose the full price, deposit amount, taxes or mandatory charges, payment schedule, cancellation/rescheduling terms and any non-refundable amounts before a customer approves the quotation or pays.

After payment

Deposits may be applied to venue reservations, suppliers, production, catering or other event costs. Whether a deposit is refundable depends on the written terms for the event and applicable law. AGE should not advertise a universal refund percentage that does not match its actual contracts.

Cancellation or rescheduling

Customers should contact AGE as soon as possible. AGE will assess the request under the applicable quotation or contract and confirm any credit, refund, rescheduling cost or supplier charge in writing.

Payment-provider refunds

Where a payment provider is used, AGE will initiate eligible refunds through the provider's supported process. Provider processing times may apply.

No hidden charges

AGE will not intentionally add undisclosed mandatory fees after approval. Any material additional service requested after the quotation should be quoted and approved separately.
