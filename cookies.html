AGE · WEBSITE POLICIES

Cookie Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026. The current GitHub Pages version is intentionally privacy-light: it does not run advertising or analytics cookies and does not load the Instagram SDK. The consent banner stores only your cookie-choice preference in browser local storage so the banner does not repeatedly appear.

Essential technology

The site uses ordinary browser features needed for navigation, accessibility and preference storage. No advertising profile is created.

Optional technology

No optional analytics, advertising or social-media tracking SDK is currently enabled. If AGE later adds one, it should be blocked until an appropriate consent choice is obtained and this policy should be updated.

Third-party links

Clicking the Instagram link or an external AGE article link takes you to another service. That service may use its own cookies and tracking under its own policies. AGE does not load those services inside the current site.

Change your choice

You can clear this site's stored browser data through your browser settings. If AGE later adds optional cookies, the consent controls should be updated to provide a way to withdraw consent.
