Our timeline

Milestones that shaped AGE.

Explore the journey by chapter. Each milestone expands without relying on hover.

1980Asia Tent ServiceThe journey begins in Gujranwala with event setup and hospitality services.

AGE traces its roots to Asia Tent Service, establishing the operational foundation that would grow into a multi-service events group.

1990Asia Party DecoratorsDécor and event styling become a dedicated part of the growing operation.

Décor becomes a dedicated capability, adding visual design and event styling to the group's growing event offering.

1998Second generation joinsMr. Abubakar Saleem Butt joins and helps take the business into its next chapter.

The second generation begins contributing to the family business, helping AGE expand its professional event-management approach.

2004Asia CateringThe group expands into professional catering and hospitality.

Asia Catering broadens the group's ability to manage food, hospitality and guest experience as part of complete events.

2013AGE Interiors & FlorasiaInterior design, décor and floral capabilities broaden the event offering.

Interior, floral and styling capabilities strengthen AGE's end-to-end event production.

2016Asia Group of Events umbrellaMezbaan Marquee, Eventurus Marquee & Event Complex and AGE Zaiqy join the expanding portfolio.

The AGE umbrella brings venues, food and youth-focused experiences into a broader family of event brands.

2018Blue Chips PRA dedicated PR and corporate communications company is launched.

Blue Chips PR adds public relations, corporate communications and activation capabilities for corporate, government and education work.

2019Events by Ruhaab & After HoursSpecialist décor and youth-focused event brands strengthen the AGE family.

Specialist brands further diversify the group's creative and entertainment offering.
