About AGE

We turn ideas into experiences and events into lasting memories.

Every event should reflect the personality, purpose and vision of the client. Our team works closely with you to design experiences that feel seamless, elegant and impactful.

Today, AGE brings event management, catering, décor, PR, venues and youth-focused experiences together through a growing family of sister brands.

Our timeline

Milestones that shaped AGE.

1980

Asia Tent Service

The journey begins in Gujranwala with event setup and hospitality services.

1990

Asia Party Decorators

Décor and event styling become a dedicated part of the growing operation.

1998

Second generation joins

Mr. Abubakar Saleem Butt joins and helps take the business into its next chapter.

2004

Asia Catering

The group expands into professional catering and hospitality.

2013

AGE Interiors & Florasia

Interior design, décor and floral capabilities broaden the event offering.

2016

Asia Group of Events umbrella

Mezbaan Marquee, Eventurus Marquee & Event Complex and AGE Zaiqy join the expanding portfolio.

2018

Blue Chips PR

A dedicated PR and corporate communications company is launched for corporate, government, education and activation work.

2019

Events by Ruhaab & After Hours

Specialist décor and youth-focused event brands strengthen the AGE family.
