AGE · WEBSITE POLICIES

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026. This website policy explains how Asia Group of Events (AGE) handles information submitted through this website. It is a general website policy and should be reviewed by AGE's legal adviser against its actual contracts, operations and applicable law before production use.

1. What we collect

AGE aims to collect only information reasonably needed for the purpose you choose. A booking enquiry may ask for your name, email, phone number, event type and basic event-planning details such as date, approximate guest count and location. A data-rights request asks for an email address and optional reference information. A blog-email preference request asks for an email address and your subscribe/unsubscribe choice.

Do not submit CNIC numbers, card numbers, passwords, financial-account credentials, precise location, children's names or dates of birth, or other sensitive information through these forms unless AGE separately and lawfully requests it.

2. Why we use information

  • To respond to event enquiries and prepare quotations.
  • To communicate about an event or service you requested.
  • To process a blog-email subscription or unsubscribe request if AGE operates such a mailing list.
  • To respond to privacy, access, correction or deletion requests.
  • To protect the website and prevent misuse where reasonably necessary.

3. Children and family events

This website does not intentionally request children's personal information. If an enquiry concerns an event involving children, please provide only the adult organiser's contact information and general event requirements. If AGE ever needs personal information about a child, it should obtain appropriate parent/guardian authorisation and collect only what is necessary for the stated purpose.

4. Sharing and third parties

AGE may share information with service providers only where reasonably necessary to provide a requested service, comply with law, protect rights, or process an approved payment. The website may load optional third-party services after a visitor chooses optional services, including Google Analytics 4, Instagram embeds, Google AdSense and external imagery. AGE should review each provider's current privacy terms and configure the integrations to match AGE's actual practices. See the Third-Party Services & SDK Audit.

5. Payments

The current payment screen on this GitHub version is a demonstration and does not process real payments. Production payments should be handled by a properly onboarded payment provider; AGE should not collect raw card details through its own static HTML pages.

6. Retention

AGE should retain enquiry and contract information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the event, business records, dispute handling, legal obligations and legitimate operational needs, then securely delete or anonymise it when no longer needed.

7. Your data rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of personal information AGE holds about you, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements. Use the Data Rights Request page or contact info@asiagroupofevents.pk.

8. Security

AGE should use appropriate administrative and technical safeguards for information it receives. This static website does not store booking form submissions in a database; the current enquiry workflow opens the visitor's email application.
